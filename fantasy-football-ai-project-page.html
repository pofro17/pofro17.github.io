Fantasy Football AI
League Assistant

An automated analytics and commentary project for a private Yahoo Fantasy Football league. The goal is to turn league data into useful, entertaining updates for our Discord community.

🏈

Live Matchup Updates

Hourly Sunday updates highlighting score changes, projected outcomes, major performances, and meaningful swings between teams.

📊

League Analytics

Position leaders, team power rankings, expected records, strength of schedule, efficiency, and other statistics beyond the standard standings.

🔥

Player Highlights

Automatically surface top QB, RB, WR, and TE performances, breakout games, disappointments, and notable fantasy events.

🤖

AI Commentary

Convert real league statistics into concise, sports-style commentary without inventing results or altering league data.

💬

Discord Delivery

Publish selected updates automatically to a private Discord channel so league members can follow the action without manual reporting.

🏆

Weekly & Season Awards

Generate weekly recaps and end-of-season awards such as MVP, best draft, waiver-wire steal, most unlucky team, and biggest collapse.

How it works

Yahoo Fantasy data→ Analytics engine→ AI analysis→ Discord

The application is intended to use read-only Yahoo Fantasy Sports data for one private league of approximately 10–12 participants. It will not modify rosters, lineups, transactions, or Yahoo Fantasy settings.

Project status: Early development. Yahoo Fantasy Sports API access is being requested for this private project. No public commercial service is planned.
